      How the ranking works

      What counts is the absolute gap between your pick and the official xG. When two picks sit the same distance away, the higher one ranks above: against a real 0.15 xG, a pick of 0.16 beats 0.14. The floor is 0.01, so hiding behind a zero for goalkeepers is off the table. Picks lock the moment the first match of the gameweek kicks off.
